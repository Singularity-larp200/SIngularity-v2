Information is processed in the United States. Account and log data is retained while an account is active and, when necessary, afterward for security or policy enforcement. information used while signed out may be stored locally on your device.

The service is not intended for children under 12, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 12. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it.

Depending on your location, you may be able to request access to or deletion of your personal information. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect some features.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy or your information, contact the service's support team through its available support channels.
