Study Tips

Spiritual habits that last

  • 1 Meditate on the Scripture you read throughout the day - reflection helps plant the Word in your heart.
  • 2 Practice reciting verses from memory. Active recall helps you rely on Scripture in your daily life.
  • 3 Engage with the Bible daily rather than only reading right before Sunday service.
  • 4 Put away your phone during quiet time. Create space to listen for God's voice without distraction.
  • 5 Share what you've learned with a friend or small group. Teaching others deeply strengthens your own faith.
  • 6 Rest is biblical. Trust God with your schedule and allow your mind and spirit to be refreshed.
